BRICK KIDS 3D - BAN V1
=======================

DAY LA PROJECT UNITY THUC, KHONG PHAI ANH MINH HOA.

Tinh nang da co trong ma nguon:
- Khoi 3D tao truc tiep bang code, co than khoi + nut tron phia tren.
- 10 loai khoi: 1x1, 1x2, 1x3, 1x4, 1x6, 2x2, 2x3, 2x4, 2x6, 2x8.
- 12 mau.
- Snap vao luoi tu dong.
- Dat khoi len tren khoi khac.
- Khong cho dat de/chong trung vi tri.
- Xoay khoi 90 do.
- 1 ngon tay: keo khoi va tha de dat.
- 2 ngon tay: xoay camera + phong to/thu nho.
- PC test: chuot trai dat khoi; chuot phai xoay; con lan zoom.
- Che do XOA: bam XOA, sau do cham khoi can xoa.
- Undo / Redo.
- Luu/mo 3 slot tren may.
- Xoa toan bo.
- Nut MAU tao nhanh mot mo hinh demo.
- Nut ANH chup anh man hinh.
- Chay offline, khong can server.

CACH MO:
1. Cai Unity Hub.
2. Cai Unity 2022.3 LTS hoac Unity 6 (kem Android Build Support neu can APK).
3. Add/Open thu muc BrickKids3D trong Unity Hub.
4. Cho Unity import va compile.
5. Project se tu tao scene Assets/BrickKidsDemo.unity.
6. Bam Play de test tren PC.

BUILD ANDROID:
- Unity menu: Brick Kids 3D > Build Android APK
- APK duoc tao tai Builds/Android/BrickKids3D.apk
- Neu Unity bao thieu Android SDK/JDK/NDK, vao Unity Hub > Installs > Add modules > Android Build Support.

LUU Y:
- May hien tai tao project khong co Unity Editor nen chua the xuat APK truc tiep trong moi truong nay.
- Project duoc viet de co the mo, test va build trong Unity.
- UI hien tai la ban V1 chuc nang; sau khi test co the nang cap giao dien tre em, icon, am thanh, hieu ung, huong dan xep theo tung buoc, xe chay/robot hoat dong.
